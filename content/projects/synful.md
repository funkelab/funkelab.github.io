[@buhmann_2021_automatic]

We developed an automatic method for synaptic partner identification in insect
brains and use it to predict synaptic partners in a whole-brain electron
microscopy dataset of the fruit fly. The predictions can be used to infer a
connectivity graph with high accuracy, thus allowing fast identification of
neural pathways. Our synapse predictions are available in the
[FlyWire](https://flywire.ai) FAFB volume.
