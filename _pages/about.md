Our lab at <a href="https://janelia.org">HHMI Janelia</a> develops machine
learning methods for the life sciences, with a focus on microscopy image
analysis.

We are particularly interested in:
1. **Identification of Structures of Interest in Large Datasets**

   In the field of connectomics, we develop methods to [segment
   neurons](projects/lsds), [detect synapses](projects/synful), and to [classify
   synapses](projects/synister) in very large electron microscopy datasets.

   We also work on the [segmentation](projects/cellulus) and [tracking of
   cells](projects/linajea) in live-cell imaging datasets.

2. **Explainable Machine Learning Methods**

   We develop methods that use machine learning to identify and visualize
   subtle patterns in biological datasets. Those methods can reveal previously
   unknown phenotypical differences, e.g., in [image data](projects/quac).

3. **Mechanistic Machine Learning**

   To increase the utility and interpretability of machine learning methods, we
   design models that directly incorporate biophysical constraints and domain
   knowledge. So far, our models have been used to [count fluorophores beyond
   the diffraction limit](projects/blinx) and to [infer synaptic plasticity
   rules](projects/plastix) from behavioral measurements.
