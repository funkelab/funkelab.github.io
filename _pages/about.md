Our lab at <a href="">HHMI Janelia</a> develops machine learning methods for
the life sciences, with a focus on microscopy image analysis.

We are particularly interested in (1) the automatic annotation of datasets that
are too large to process by hand (e.g., [Neuron
Segmentation](projects/synister) and [Synapse Detection](projects/synful) in
connectomics datasets, and [Cell Tracking](projects/linajea) in 3D+t movies),
(2) explainable machine learning methods that reveal previously unknown
phenological differences in image data ([QuAC](projects/quac)), and (3)
mechanistic machine learning models to infer and explain complex observations
(e.g. [blinx](projects/blinx) to count fluorophores and
[plastix](projects/plastix) to infer plasticity rules from behavior).
